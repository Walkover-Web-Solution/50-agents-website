Questions people ask

What is an AI agent builder?

A tool that turns a description of a job into software that does the job in your apps on its own schedule. In 50agents the builder is Captain: you describe the job, it asks what it cannot guess, shows a plan, and builds the AI agent when you tap Build it. How to choose an AI agent builder lists the questions to ask.

Can I build an AI agent without code?

Yes. Everything is in plain words: the ask, Captain's plan, the AI agent's instructions and the changes you make later in its chat. There is no canvas to wire and no code to write.

Will an AI agent send emails without asking me?

Not by default. Reading runs on its own; sending, updating a record or changing access waits in Needs you for one tap. AI agents ask before sending, even to you, until you allow it in Settings.

AI agent or automation: which do I need?

If the steps are the same every time, an automation: it uses AI only in a step that needs it, and it can start on an app event. If each run needs a decision, such as which deals are worth a follow-up, an AI agent. Captain picks and tells you which. See AI agent vs automation for the full comparison.

What can't an AI agent do yet?

It cannot start the moment a new email arrives; it runs on a schedule or when you ask it in its chat. It has no email address of its own and sends from the apps you connect, and Undo covers its own setup, not what it changed in your outside apps.

How is this different from Lindy or Zapier?

Lindy is built around one AI assistant you hire, and in Zapier you set up each AI agent yourself. In 50agents, Captain plans the AI agent from your sentence and you approve the plan; Lindy states a SOC 2 report and Zapier has a larger app directory, and 50agents holds no security certification. See 50agents vs Lindy and Gumloop alternatives.

Is there a free AI agent builder?

50agents is free to start. Describe the job to Captain, review its plan, and tap Build it when you are happy with it.

Which apps can an AI agent use?

It works with 2,300+ apps through viaSocket, such as HubSpot, Gmail, Slack, Stripe and Google Sheets. Captain asks which apps you use while it plans, and the AI agent works only in the apps you connect.

How does an AI agent know about my business?

Your workspace remembers facts about the business, such as hours, prices, policies, people and tone, plus the documents, pasted text and links you give it. AI agents answer from these, so a drafted reply sounds like you.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

You correct it, and the lesson stays in its own memory with its instructions and its last runs. You can see, correct or forget any item there. Anything that reaches someone outside the business waits in Needs you, so a mistake is caught before it is sent.
