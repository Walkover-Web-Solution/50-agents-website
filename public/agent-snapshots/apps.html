Questions people ask

Can I build an app without code?

Yes. You describe it, Captain asks what the app is for and which apps you use today, then shows the plan. Build it makes the app from parts that are already tested, so nothing is code written by the AI.

Can I build an app from Google Sheets?

You can bring a sheet in: download it as CSV or Excel, drop it into the app and check where each column goes. The app does not stay linked to the sheet; for a live link, Glide or Softr fit better.

What is an internal tool, and is this one?

An internal tool is software your team uses to run the business, not something customers see: a pipeline, a requests desk, an invoice tracker. A 50agents app is exactly that, with AI agents doing the routine work in it. Internal tools without code: what to build first helps you pick.

Who does the work in the app?

Its crew. In the leads app, Closer adds new leads from your email and drafts follow-ups for quiet ones, and Scout, a browser agent, reads each new lead's company website. A duty that emails people outside always asks first.

What can't an app do yet?

It cannot stay linked to a Google Sheet or show charts or a calendar yet. It does not sync with an accounting or CRM app unless an automation is built for it.

Can I change the app after it is built?

Yes, by asking in its chat: add a field, a screen or a duty. Each change is a version with Undo, and removing a field never deletes the records behind it.

Is it free to start?

Yes. 50agents is free to start: describe the app to Captain, check the plan and tap Build it.

How do I get my records in?

Add them by hand, paste a table, or drop a CSV or Excel file and Captain matches the columns to the fields. A file import keeps one record per email address today.

What screens does an app have?

Lists of records with typed fields, shown as a board grouped by a choice field with totals, or as a table you can search and sort. The Overview shows the numbers that matter.

When do the AI agents in an app act?

On a record event: a record added, a field becoming a value, or a date passing by a number of days. A duty that emails someone outside the business waits in Needs you: Approve and send, Change, or Decline.
