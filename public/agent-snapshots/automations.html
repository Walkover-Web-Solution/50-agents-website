Questions people ask

What is workflow automation for a small business?

Software that does a repeated job between your apps without you: every new order or payment logged, every new HubSpot contact posted to Slack, every Friday report sent. Start with a job you do the same way at least weekly, where a mistake is cheap to fix.

How do I start an automation in 50agents?

Say what should happen, for example "every new Shopify order is added as a row to my Google Sheet with its items and total". Captain asks only what it cannot guess, such as which sheet, plans the steps, tests them on your real data and leaves the automation as a draft until you make it live.

Does every run use AI?

No. The fixed steps run without AI. An AI step is added only where a step needs judgement, such as sorting an email, and when that step is unsure the case goes to the automation working as an AI agent.

What happens when a step fails?

Every step after a failed one is skipped, so nothing goes out after a mistake. The run shows the error in plain words, with Fix with Captain, and Care is told.

Will an automation email my customers without asking?

No. A send to anyone other than you asks first on every run, in Needs you. Writes to your own tools, such as a row in your sheet, run on their own; a post to a Slack channel also waits for approval today.

How is this different from Zapier, Make or n8n?

In those you build each step yourself, on a list or a canvas; in 50agents Captain builds it from your sentence and tests it on your data. Zapier has a larger app directory, Make has a visual canvas, and n8n can run on your own server, which 50agents cannot. Zapier vs Make vs n8n for a small team compares those three.

Is it free to start?

Yes. 50agents is free to start: describe the job to Captain, review its plan and tap Build it when you are happy with it.

Which apps can an automation connect?

It works with 2,300+ apps through viaSocket, such as Stripe, Shopify, HubSpot, Google Sheets and Slack. Captain names the apps in its plan, and you connect each one it needs.

What can start an automation?

An event in one of your apps, such as a new contact, a new payment or a new order, or a schedule, such as every Friday morning. The same steps then run each time.

Can I see what an automation has done?

Yes. Each automation has its own memory: its instructions, what it learned, lessons from your corrections and its last runs. You can see, correct or forget any item.
