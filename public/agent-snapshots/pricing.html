Questions about pricing

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every workspace gets 2,000 free credits every month, with every kind of AI agent, automation and app. They end with the month and come back fresh.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Work waits rather than failing, and one item in Needs you says so. Top up or move to a plan, and it carries on.

Do unused credits roll over?

Free and plan credits end with the month. Top-ups never expire, and plan credits are used before them.

How do top-ups work?

Pay as you go at $1 per 1,000 credits, any whole thousand from 5,000. They stay until you use them.

How do I upgrade?

In the app, open Settings, then Usage, and pick a plan. You add a card once and pay; the credits arrive when the payment goes through.

Does a plan replace the free credits?

Yes. A plan’s monthly credits take the place of the free 2,000 for as long as the plan is paid.

Can I see what used my credits?

Yes. Usage in Settings shows every charge: who used it, on what, and when, with a CSV download.
