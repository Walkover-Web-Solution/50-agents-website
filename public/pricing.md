# 50agents pricing

Every workspace starts free with **2,000 credits**. Paid usage is metered in credits; meeting recording is billed separately by hour.

## AI Note Taker

- **$0.99 per hour** of recorded meeting time.
- No monthly commitment; pay only for hours you record.

## Internal applications and AI agents

- **Free for the first 1,000 API calls per month** per workspace.
- Standard metered pricing applies after 1,000 calls (see the product workspace for current rates).

## Workspace credits (platform)

- Starter allowance: **2,000 credits** included at signup.
- Credits cover Captain-built automations, AI agents, and workflow runs across connected apps.
